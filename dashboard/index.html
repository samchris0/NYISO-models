READING THE RESULTS

A fair comparison

By default, models are scored only where every selected run has a successful, evaluated prediction for the same origin and target. Changing the models may therefore change the scoring sample.

Bias is prediction minus actual: negative means underprediction. Coverage counts stored jobs, not all theoretically expected schedule slots. Dates include the full selected New York calendar days.

Historical runs are retrospective backtests. Their scores do not establish that source observations or trained artifacts were available in real time. Recorded live forecasts are shown separately.
